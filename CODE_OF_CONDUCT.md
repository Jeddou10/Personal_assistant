# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming. Discuss ideas and behavior rather than attacking people. Contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels are welcome.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, impersonation, and publishing someone's private information without permission are not acceptable. Do not post credentials or private mailbox, calendar, or document content in discussions or examples.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. They should explain decisions where doing so does not expose confidential information, and apply these expectations consistently.

For a conduct concern, use GitHub's **Report content** control on the relevant issue, comment, or profile. If a concern can be raised without disclosing private information, you may also ask a repository maintainer for a private contact route. Do not publish another person's sensitive details in order to make a report.

This policy applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.
